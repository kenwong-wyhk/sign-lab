01

Watch them cancel

Every number is a term being added. Green tiles are +1, red tiles −1; they cancel one pair at a time, and the leftover colour is the sign. The same tiles handle x: 4x − 6x leaves two red x-tiles, so −2x.

02

Pull the terms apart

18 − 4x + 8x − 7 is four terms: +18, −4x, +8x and −7. The sign belongs to the term, not to the operation in front of it.
